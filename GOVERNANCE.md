Governance of this project
===========================

This is a personal project of Dai Mikurube ([@dmikurube](https://github.com/dmikurube)). It does not actively look for outside contributions.

[@dmikurube](https://github.com/dmikurube) may invite collaborators to this project. The collaborators may submit pull requests, but [@dmikurube](https://github.com/dmikurube) will make all decisions about merging them.
